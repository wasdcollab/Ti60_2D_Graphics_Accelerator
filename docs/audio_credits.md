# Audio credits and redistribution notes

The FPGA sound-effect ROM is derived from four files by Taira Komori:
`shoot2`, `laser_beam`, `game_explosion3`, and `large_destruction1`, from the
weapons/explosions collection.  The site permits use in games and product
embedding, but prohibits redistributing the sound materials themselves.  Raw
MP3 inputs are therefore local build inputs and must not be committed or
distributed with this repository.  Source: https://taira-komori.net/arms01tw.html

The desired public Touhou-related BGM candidate is *Letter to the Witch OST*
by PetarS, released CC0.  It needs an accessible download plus a separate
streaming path before it can replace the FPGA's procedural BGM.
